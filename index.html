Click or tap the cells to try it now, or turn on the camera to play in the air.

Three moves. That's the whole lesson.

1

Hold up your hands

Sit about an arm's length from the screen in good light. Both hands are tracked at once, up to 30 times a second.

2

Pinch to play

Touch your thumb to your index finger inside a cell. Left is low, right is high. Every note fits the scale, so nothing sounds wrong.

3

Loop and layer

Press Loop, play two bars, and it repeats. Add drums on top, then a melody. Record a clip to share.
